# Image Prompts — Water Softener St George Utah

28 images: 3 homepage, 21 inner-page headers, 4 About-page body images.
Works with Gemini / Imagen, Midjourney, Flux, DALL-E, Ideogram.

## How to use

1. Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** first (or append it to the end of each prompt).
2. Paste the image-specific prompt.
3. Save the result using the **filename** shown, as `.webp`, into `src/assets/images/`.
4. Tell Claude "wire the images" and it will import and place them.

Set aspect ratio per group: homepage hero **4:3**, homepage tiles **3:2**, page headers **5:4**, About banners **16:9**.
Generate at 2x the display size or larger. Convert to WebP afterwards (quality ~80).

## STYLE BLOCK (use with every image)

```
Photorealistic editorial photograph, natural daylight, shallow depth of field,
shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens. Southern Utah / St George setting:
red-rock sandstone, warm desert light, clear blue sky, stucco and tile-roof
homes, xeriscaped yards. Colour grade: clean and slightly cool in the shadows
with warm terracotta accents; deep navy blue (#17496B) and terracotta (#C2562B)
appear naturally in the scene where possible. Professional local-trades-business
feel, honest and credible, not stock-photo glossy. Generic unbranded equipment
only. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand names on products, no
cartoon or clipart look, no smiling family holding glasses of water, no
exaggerated before/after splits.
```

## NEGATIVE PROMPT (for tools that support one)

```
text, letters, logos, watermark, brand names, cartoon, illustration, clipart,
3D render, plastic skin, extra fingers, distorted hands, blurry, oversaturated,
stock-photo smiling family, glass of water held up to camera
```

## Tips

- Prompts #11, #12, #17 and #18 involve equipment details. If a generator draws gibberish labels or odd fittings, regenerate or add "simple, clean, minimal labelling" to the prompt.
- Real photos of your own installs will outperform AI images for local trust once a tenant signs. Swap them in later using the same filenames.
- Keep faces out of focus or turned away on the technician shots; it avoids the uncanny-face problem and keeps the site feeling local rather than staged.
