## What Water Softener Repairs Are Available in St George, UT?

Seven repairs cover the common failures: control valve repair, motor [replacement](/water-softener-replacement/), float valve repair, injector cleaning, head replacement, brine line repair and leak repair.

### Water Softener Control Valve Repair

The control valve directs water through the service, backwash, brine and rinse stages. When it sticks, leaks or fails to cycle, water stays hard or runs constantly to the drain. The technician rebuilds the valve with new seals and spacers.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Water Softener Motor Replacement St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Water Softener Motor Replacement

The motor turns the valve through its regeneration cycle. A failed motor leaves the unit stuck in one stage, so hard water reaches the house. The technician replaces the motor and gear and tests a full cycle.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Float Valve Repair St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Float Valve Repair

The float valve controls the water level in the brine tank. A stuck float lets the tank overflow or run short of brine. The technician cleans or replaces it and sets the level.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Injector Cleaning St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Injector Cleaning

The injector draws brine into the resin tank. Grit blocks it, and the unit then fails to recharge, so water turns hard even with salt in the tank. The technician cleans or replaces the injector and its screen.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Head Replacement St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Head Replacement

The control head holds the timer and valve. When it fails, repair costs more than a new head, and replacing the head restores the cycle while keeping the tank. The technician matches the head to your tank.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Brine Line Repair St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Brine Line Repair

The brine line carries salty water from the brine tank to the resin tank. A cracked or blocked line stops regeneration. The technician replaces the tubing and fittings and checks for leaks.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Water Softener Leak Repair St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Water Softener Leak Repair

Leaks come from loose fittings, worn seals, a cracked drain line or a failing tank. The technician finds the source, repairs or replaces the part, and tells you if the tank itself has failed.

</div>

## Common Water Softener Problems We Fix

- **Not regenerating:** the unit never starts a cycle because of a power, timer, motor or valve fault.
- **Salt bridge:** a hard crust forms above the water in the brine tank, so salt cannot dissolve. The technician breaks it up and cleans the tank.
- **Mushing:** salt dissolves and re-forms into sludge at the bottom, blocking brine flow. The technician empties and cleans the tank.
- **Error codes:** meanings differ by brand. The technician reads the code, finds the cause and clears it.
