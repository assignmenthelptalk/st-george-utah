## What Decides the Cost of a Water Softener?

Five factors move the price: system type, grain capacity, installation work, extras and ongoing costs.

- **System type:** a single-tank salt-based unit costs less than a [dual tank system](/dual-tank-water-softener/), and a salt-free conditioner has a different cost profile again.
- **Grain capacity:** larger households and harder water need more capacity, and capacity is sized from your hardness reading.
- **Installation:** a unit set beside an existing drain and outlet costs less than one that needs new plumbing, a drain line or power.
- **Extras:** bypass valves, pressure regulators, sediment pre-filters and iron filters add to the total.
- **Ongoing costs:** salt, water for regeneration and a yearly service visit continue after installation.

## How Does St George Water Affect the Price?

Harder water needs more capacity or more frequent regeneration. At 13 to 24 gpg, a household of four needs 3,900 to 7,200 grains per day, so size and salt use run higher than in softer-water cities.
