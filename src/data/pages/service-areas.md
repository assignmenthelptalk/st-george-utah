### St George

St George homes receive City of St. George water, a mixture of Virgin River surface water bought from the Washington County Water Conservancy District and City groundwater for part of the year. The City's reports advise softener settings of 13 to 24 GPG (about 222 to 410 mg/L) <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: the City's reports give a softener-setting range, not a measured hardness]</mark>.
