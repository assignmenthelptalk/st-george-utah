## How Does UV Water Purification Work in St George, UT?

UV purification covers three topics: how UV works, UV system installation and combining UV with a water softener.

### How UV Water Purification Works

A UV system passes water through a chamber with an ultraviolet lamp that damages the DNA of bacteria, viruses and cysts so they cannot reproduce. It adds nothing to the water and does not change taste. The water needs to be clear for UV to work, so a sediment filter goes ahead of it.

### UV and Water Softener Combination

UV does not soften water, and hardness can foul the quartz sleeve around the lamp, so a softener upstream keeps the sleeve clean. Hard well water often needs both, and [well water](/well-water-softener-st-george-ut/) testing shows which.

</div>

### Ideal for Well Water in St George

Private wells have no utility disinfection, so a bacteria test decides whether UV is needed. <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: cite a recommended private-well bacteria testing frequency.]</mark> UV is a common final step after sediment and iron filters on a well.

> **Not a good fit if...** your water needs a chemical contaminant removed. UV treats living organisms only, and a certified filter or reverse osmosis handles chemicals.
