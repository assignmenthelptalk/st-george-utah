## Why Is St George Water So Hard?

St George water is hard because both of its sources pick up minerals in Southern Utah rock.

### Where Does St George Water Come From?

According to the City of St. George's 2023 and 2024 water quality reports, the City buys Virgin River surface water from the Washington County Water Conservancy District, treated at the Quail Creek Water Treatment Plant, and also runs groundwater wells and springs. Customers receive a mixture of the two for part of the year, and the reports say most Southern Utah sources are hard because of calcium and magnesium.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Hard Is St George Water?

The City of St. George's reports advise softener settings of 13 to 24 grains per gallon, about 222 to 410 mg/L <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: the City's reports give a softener-setting range, not a measured hardness]</mark>. The reports do not give a single measured hardness, so a tap test at your address is the reliable number.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Is Water Hardness Measured?

Hardness is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) or grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The U.S. Geological Survey rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 mg/L as moderately hard, 121 to 180 mg/L as hard and anything over 180 mg/L as very hard. By that scale, water at 13 to 24 gpg is very hard.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### What Does Hard Water Do to a Home?

Hard water leaves marks across the whole house. Scale builds inside water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines, narrows pipes, clogs showerheads and leaves white crust on faucets. Hard water also reacts with soap to form a film that leaves skin dry and hair dull, and minerals stiffen laundry. Newer homes with tankless water heaters and high-end dishwashers see the damage in dollars.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Do You Find Your Exact Hardness?

A free water test shows the exact reading at your tap, because the season's blend of surface water and groundwater and your home plumbing both move the number. The technician records it in grains per gallon and sizes the softener to that figure.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Does a Water Softener Fix Hard Water?

A water softener removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange and replaces them with sodium or potassium, so scale stops forming and soap lathers properly. For St George homeowners, that single change protects the water heater, the plumbing and the fixtures at the same time.
