### What Is a Salt-Free Water Softener System?

A salt-free water softener system is a [conditioner](/water-conditioner-installation/) that changes how hardness minerals behave instead of removing them. Calcium and magnesium stay in the water, but they are less likely to form hard scale. The unit installs on the main line and has no regeneration cycle. Because it adds no sodium, it suits homeowners watching salt intake.

St George water is very hard at 13 to 24 GPG. A salt-free system reduces scale in that water but does not give the slick, mineral-free feel of a [salt-based](/water-softener-guides/salt-vs-salt-free-water-softener/) softener. ### Template Assisted Crystallization (TAC) Systems

A template assisted crystallization (TAC) system passes water over specially made media that turns dissolved hardness minerals into microscopic crystals. Those crystals stay suspended and resist attaching to pipes, heaters and fixtures. TAC is the most widely used salt-free method, and the media is replaced on a schedule that depends on water use. ### Electronic Descaler Installation

An electronic descaler wraps a coil around the main pipe and sends a changing electronic signal through the water. It installs in under an hour without cutting pipe <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY by model]</mark>. ### Magnetic Water Conditioner Installation

A magnetic water conditioner clamps magnets onto the pipe to alter how minerals form scale. It is the lowest-cost, lowest-maintenance option. Independent evidence for magnetic conditioners is limited, and they fit only where a light scale reduction is the goal.

### Salt-Free vs. Salt-Based: Which Is Right for St George Water?

A salt-based softener removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange and delivers true soft water. A salt-free system reduces scale but leaves the minerals in place.

- **Choose salt-based** if your water is very hard (above 180 mg/L), you want soft-feeling water, or spotting on glass and fixtures bothers you most.
- **Choose salt-free** if you want no salt, no drain connection and minimal upkeep, and scale protection is the main goal.

> **Not a good fit if...** your water carries iron or sulfur (these need filtration first) or you want fully softened water for skin, hair and laundry. A free test at your tap decides it.
