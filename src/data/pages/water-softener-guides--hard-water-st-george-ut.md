## What Do St George Homeowners Need to Know About Hard Water?

Three questions cover the basics: what hard water is, why St George water is hard and how hard it measures.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">


![What Is Hard Water? St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### What Is Hard Water?

Hard water contains dissolved calcium and magnesium. It is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) or grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The USGS rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 mg/L as moderately hard, 121 to 180 mg/L as hard and over 180 mg/L as very hard.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Why Is St George Water Hard? St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Why Is St George Water Hard?

St George water is hard because both of its sources pick up minerals. The Virgin River and the City's groundwater wells and springs run through Southern Utah rock, and the City's reports say most Southern Utah sources are hard because of calcium and magnesium.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![How Hard Is St George Water? St George UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### How Hard Is St George Water?

The City of St. George's 2023 and 2024 water quality reports advise softener settings of 13 to 24 GPG, about 222 to 410 mg/L at 17.1 mg/L per gpg. <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: the City's reports give a softener-setting range, not a measured hardness]</mark> The reports say customers receive a mixture of groundwater and surface water for part of the year, so the reading at your tap depends on the blend and the season.

</div>

</div>

## What Hard Water Does to a Home

Hard water leaves scale in water heaters, dishwashers, washing machines, pipes and fixtures, and it reacts with soap to leave a film on skin, hair and laundry. Scale narrows pipes over time and makes appliances work harder.

## Solutions for Hard Water

A [salt-based](/water-softener-guides/salt-vs-salt-free-water-softener/) water softener removes the hardness minerals, a [salt-free conditioner](/salt-free-water-softener-systems/) changes how scale forms, and a filter does not address hardness. For the strongest result in water above 180 mg/L, a correctly sized salt-based softener is the standard answer. For installation, see [Water Softener Installation in St George, UT](/water-softener-installation/).

> **Not a good fit if...** your tap tests under 60 mg/L. That water is soft, and a softener adds no benefit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq-list">

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is St George water hard?</summary>

Yes. The City of St. George advises softener settings of 13 to 24 GPG, which is Very Hard on the WQA scale above 10.5 GPG, so scale builds quickly in water heaters and on fixtures.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is hard water bad for your health?</summary>

Hard water is not a health risk in the amounts found in St George. It affects plumbing, appliances, skin and laundry rather than drinking safety.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Why is my neighbor's water harder than mine?</summary>

The City's supply is a mixture of Virgin River surface water and groundwater for part of the year, and the pipes in your home change what reaches the tap. A test shows your reading.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does boiling remove water hardness?</summary>

Boiling precipitates some of the temporary hardness as scale, and it does not make hard water soft. A softener removes the minerals before they reach the pipes.
