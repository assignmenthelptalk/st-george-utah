### Lead and Arsenic Removal

Lead usually enters water from service lines and home plumbing, not from the utility's source. A certified filter or [reverse osmosis system](/reverse-osmosis-installation/) reduces lead and arsenic at the tap. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: lead service line inventory from your provider.]</mark> Check your service line material as well as filtering.

### Fluoride Filter Installation

Fluoride is present naturally or added by some utilities. A reverse osmosis or specialty media filter reduces fluoride at the tap for homeowners who want it lower. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: fluoride level from your provider's current report.]</mark>

### PFAS Water Filter

PFAS are a group of persistent chemicals, and a carbon block or reverse osmosis filter certified for PFAS reduces them at the tap. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: PFAS results from your provider's current report.]</mark>

</div>

### Nitrate Filter Installation

Nitrate is a concern mainly for [private wells](/well-water-softener-st-george-ut/) and for infants, and an ion exchange or reverse osmosis system reduces it. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: nitrate result from your provider's report against the 10 ppm limit.]</mark>

</div>

### VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) Filter

A carbon filter reduces volatile organic compounds, which come from solvents and fuels. A water test shows whether any are present before a filter is chosen.

> **Not a good fit if...** your test shows no contaminant of concern. A filter adds cost without benefit, and the technician tells you so.
