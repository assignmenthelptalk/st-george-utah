## What Are the Signs That a St George Water Softener Needs Replacing?

Seven signs show a softener is failing, and the first six are listed below.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">



![Hard-Water Symptoms Return St George UT](../../assets/images/card-hard-water-symptoms-return-st-george-ut.webp)



### 1. Hard-Water Symptoms Return

White crust on faucets, spotted glass and stiff laundry mean the unit is not softening. **Check:** run a [hardness test](/water-quality-testing/) on softened water. A reading above 1 gpg points to failed resin or a failed valve <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: typical softened-water target]</mark>.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Salt Use Rises Sharply St George UT](../../assets/images/card-salt-use-rises-sharply-st-george-ut.webp)



### 2. Salt Use Rises Sharply

A unit that suddenly burns through salt may have a stuck valve, a wrong setting or worn resin. **Check:** note how often you refill and compare it with the past.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Salt Bridges or Mush Keep Returning St George UT](../../assets/images/card-salt-bridges-or-mush-keep-returning-st-george-ut.webp)



### 3. Salt Bridges or Mush Keep Returning

A hard crust or sludge in the brine tank blocks brine from reaching the resin. Cleaning helps, and repeat cases point to a worn tank or the wrong salt.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Leaks From the Tank, Head or Drain St George UT](../../assets/images/card-leaks-from-the-tank-head-or-drain-st-george-ut.webp)



### 4. Leaks From the Tank, Head or Drain

Leaks from fittings or seals are repairable. A leak from the tank itself means replacement. See [Water Softener Repair in St George, UT](/water-softener-repair/) for what is fixable.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Error Codes or No Cycling St George UT](../../assets/images/card-error-codes-or-no-cycling-st-george-ut.webp)



### 5. Error Codes or No Cycling

A control head that shows error codes or never starts a cycle has a failed motor, valve or board. The technician reads the code and prices a repair against a new head.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Resin Beads in Faucets or Sinks St George UT](../../assets/images/card-resin-beads-in-faucets-or-sinks-st-george-ut.webp)



### 6. Resin Beads in Faucets or Sinks

Small tan beads in the aerators mean the resin tank's internal parts have failed. This calls for repair or replacement right away.

</div>

</div>

### 7. The Unit Is Past 10 to 15 Years Old

Industry guidance commonly puts softener life at [10 to 15 years](/water-softener-guides/how-long-do-water-softeners-last/) <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: cite source]</mark>. A unit near that age with one of the signs above usually costs less to replace than to keep repairing. See [Water Softener Replacement in St George, UT](/water-softener-replacement/).
