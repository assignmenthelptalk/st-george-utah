## Full Water Softener System Replacement

A full replacement removes the old tank, brine tank and control head and installs a new complete system. It fits units with a leaking or cracked tank, a failed control head that costs more to fix than to replace, or capacity that no longer matches the household. The technician reuses sound plumbing and replaces worn fittings.

## Resin Bed Replacement

Resin bed replacement swaps the resin beads inside the tank while keeping the tank and control head. Worn resin stops removing hardness, so water turns hard even though the softener runs and uses salt. It suits a sound tank with an old resin bed, and the technician tests the resin before recommending it.

## Water Softener Upgrade Service

An upgrade replaces an undersized or outdated unit with a larger or more efficient one. It fits a household that has grown or a home on harder water than the original unit was sized for. At 13 to 24 gpg a 48,000-grain unit suits a household of four, and a unit sized for 7 gpg water falls short.

## Repair or Replace?

Repair fits a young unit with one failed part, and replacement fits an old unit, a leaking tank or repeated failures.
