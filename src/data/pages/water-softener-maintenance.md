## What Does Water Softener Maintenance Include in St George, UT?

Four services keep a softener at full capacity: resin cleaning, salt refills, brine tank cleaning and annual maintenance plans.

### Water Softener Resin Cleaning Service

Resin beads collect iron and sediment over time and lose capacity. Cleaning the resin with a softener cleaner restores it, which matters most for homes on well water or older pipes. The technician tests the result afterward.

### Water Softener Salt Refill Service

A salt refill delivers and loads the correct salt for your unit, so the tank never runs dry. Running out of salt leaves the water hard until the tank is refilled. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: tenant's salt refill pricing.]</mark>

### Water Softener Brine Tank Cleaning

Salt bridges and mushing form in the brine tank and stop brine from reaching the resin. The technician empties the tank, removes the sludge and refills it with clean salt.

## How Often Should You Service a Softener in St George?

Check salt monthly and schedule a technician visit once a year. The City of St. George advises softener settings of 13 to 24 GPG, and very hard water uses salt and resin capacity faster. A hardness test of the softened water shows when service is due: a reading above 1 gpg means the system is not keeping up <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: typical softened-water target]</mark>.

> **Not a good fit if...** your unit is past about 10 to 15 years old <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: cite source]</mark> and needs repeated repairs. Replacement is the better spend.


## When does maintenance stop being enough?

Maintenance fixes salt, resin and brine problems. A failed valve, motor or tank needs repair or [replacement](/water-softener-replacement/), which the technician spots on the first visit.

<summary>What kind of salt should I use?</summary>
