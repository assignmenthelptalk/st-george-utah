### What Is a Dual Tank Water Softener System?

A dual tank water softener is a twin-tank system in which two resin tanks share the softening work. While Tank A softens the water you use, Tank B regenerates, then the roles switch. A single-tank softener pauses softening during regeneration and sends [hard water](/water-softener-guides/hard-water-st-george-ut/) to the house until the cycle ends. A dual tank system removes that gap, and many designs regenerate only when a tank is actually exhausted, which uses salt and water in line with real demand.

### Benefits for High-Usage St George Homes

Hard water is a daily fact for St George households: water calls for softener settings of 13 to 24 GPG (about 222 to 410 mg/L) <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: the City's reports give a softener-setting range, not a measured hardness]</mark>, which is very hard. Dual tank systems help most where water use is high:

- **Large families** with several showers, loads of laundry and a dishwasher running in the same hours.
- **Homes with multiple bathrooms, pools or irrigation draws** that put heavy demand on a single tank.
- **Households with night-time or early-morning peaks**, since regeneration no longer interrupts softened water.

### Commercial Dual Tank Systems

Businesses that need soft water around the clock benefit most from two tanks. Restaurants, hotels, laundries and car washes run equipment where even a short stretch of hard water causes scale in boilers, steamers and spray nozzles. A commercial dual tank system is sized to peak flow rate, not household size.
